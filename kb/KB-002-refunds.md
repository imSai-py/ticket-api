# KB-002: Refund policy

You can request a refund for product purchases within 14 days of the purchase date. (For recurring subscription plans and billing disputes, see KB-003 for the 30-day dispute window).

- Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear.
- Digital items that have been downloaded or activated are not refundable.
- Partial refunds are not offered. If you need a partial adjustment, contact support.
- To request a refund, reply to your order confirmation email with the order number.
