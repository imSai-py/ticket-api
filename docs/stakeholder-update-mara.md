# Stakeholder update

## Progress
We walked through the billing escalation tool together today. Support agents can now enter customer billing problems on a simple web page, and the system automatically drafts a response, tags urgency, and records refund amounts in your tracking spreadsheet. Managers receive emails with quick buttons to approve or reject refunds. Clicking a button updates the spreadsheet immediately and notifies the support agent by email.

## What changed
Following your feedback in today's demo, we are adding a third "Need more info" button alongside Approve and Reject. We also set our two daily digest email times: 9:00 AM and 3:00 PM. All dates now display in Day/Month/Year (`DD/MM/YYYY`) format. This week's release focuses strictly on billing issues, with other categories queued for next week.

## Risks
If someone leaves the tracking spreadsheet open in desktop Excel when an approval comes through, updates will pause until they close the file. As agreed in the demo, we are keeping this simple workflow for now rather than taking two to three days to rebuild around Google Sheets.
